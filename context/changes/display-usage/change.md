## Notes

Display the total daily usage cost of AI to generate covers. Place it on the web page and also on the display itself. On the display it should be shown only at the time of new cover generation in the form of a small bar at the bottom of the frame. The webpage should have a limit set and this bar should be scaled for that. Also this limit should be respected and if you exceed it do not generate AI frames anymore.
